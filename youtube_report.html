📋 How to Use This Analysis

1. Topic Research: Find topics with high search_volume (>1000/month) and low competition (<500 videos).
2. Demand/Supply Score: Calculate demand_supply = search_volume / competition. Score > 5 = good opportunity.
3. Title Optimization: Start with "How to", include a number (e.g. "5 Ways to..."), keep length 50-70 chars.
4. Video Length: Aim for 10-15 minutes — optimal for watch time and algorithm.
5. Publishing Time: Best day: Wed | Best hours: 1:00, 13:00, 6:00
6. Thumbnail: Include a face — increases CTR by ~15%.
